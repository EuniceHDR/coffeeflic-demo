# CoffeefliC | Coffee Ordering App UI/UX Prototype

**A mobile-first coffee ordering experience designed to make choosing, customizing, and ordering a drink feel simple and intuitive.**

[**Explore the Interactive Figma Prototype**](https://www.figma.com/proto/3vYd4slqbkcgEsn7BZgn9p/Prototype-Practice?node-id=245-182&starting-point-node-id=155%3A68)

## Project Overview

CoffeefliC is a self-initiated UI/UX design project exploring the customer journey of a mobile coffee ordering app. The prototype brings together coffee discovery, product options, cart review, and delivery-related screens in a connected Figma experience.

**Project type:** Mobile app UI/UX design and interactive prototyping  
**Design tool:** Figma  
**Focus:** Navigation, ordering flow, usability, and visual consistency  
**Status:** Figma prototype / portfolio demonstration

## Experience Highlights

- **Coffee discovery:** Browse coffee offerings and explore product screens.
- **Product customization:** Present product choices before an item is added to the order.
- **Cart experience:** Guide customers through reviewing their selections.
- **Address and delivery flow:** Explore the steps for supplying delivery information.
- **Interactive navigation:** Connect screens and key actions using Figma prototyping.

## Design Approach

The concept focuses on a clear visual hierarchy, easy-to-find actions, consistent components, and a straightforward ordering sequence. The goal is to reduce friction as users move from selecting a coffee to preparing an order.
