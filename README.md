## UX decisions

- **Clear wayfinding:** Consistent navigation between home, discovery, saved drinks, cart, and account areas.
- **Personalized choices:** Size and milk selections are presented at the point of decision.
- **Order transparency:** Cart screens show selections, quantities, fees, and totals before confirmation.
- **Flexible fulfillment:** Pickup and delivery are treated as separate paths.
- **Post-order clarity:** Confirmation and status screens illustrate the next steps after an order.
